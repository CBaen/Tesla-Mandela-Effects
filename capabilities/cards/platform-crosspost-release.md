# Platform Cross-Post Release

## What It Does

Keeps cross-platform release work from becoming a blind YouTube metadata copy.
Use it when completed Tesla Mandela Effects episodes are being moved from
YouTube into another video or podcast platform.

## Current Evidence

- First three YouTube episodes are public.
- Public YouTube data review was recorded in
  `../../14. RUMBLE SPOTIFY LAUNCH PACKAGE/youtube-data-review-2026-05-29.md`.
- Rumble/Spotify profile copy and episode metadata were specialized in
  `../../14. RUMBLE SPOTIFY LAUNCH PACKAGE/profile-copy.md` and
  `../../14. RUMBLE SPOTIFY LAUNCH PACKAGE/episode-metadata.md`.
- Upload-safe Rumble and Spotify derivatives were generated and verified in
  `../../14. RUMBLE SPOTIFY LAUNCH PACKAGE/delivery-summary-2026-05-29.md`.
- Live Rumble/Spotify publishing was not performed because no safe
  authenticated browser/API path was available.

## Rules

- Verify active platform account identity before editing.
- Use platform-specific copy, not direct YouTube copy.
- Lead with searchable entities and the mystery hook.
- Keep the docufiction boundary visible.
- Do not represent fictional archive fragments as verified real sources.
- Do not copy the YouTube Episode 003 `S1E1` metadata bug.
- Keep generated media out of Git.
