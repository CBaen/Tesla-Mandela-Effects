## Framing Rules for All Public Copy

### USE:
- "Investigation," "explores," "asks whether," "traces," "follows"
- "The record shows," "the data suggests," "the question was never answered"
- "Docufiction," "investigative storytelling," "verified fact alongside constructed sources"
- Open-ended questions: "Was it discovered — or rediscovered?"

### AVOID:
- "Conspiracy," "cover-up," "they don't want you to know," "exposed," "revealed"
- "The truth about," "wake up," "sheeple," "mainstream lies"
- Definitive claims about institutional malice: "NASA hid," "the government destroyed"
- Any framing that promises revelation rather than investigation

### WHY:
The word "conspiracy" isn't algorithmically suppressed — but it selects for an audience that bounces quickly, which teaches YouTube's algorithm the content underperforms. Investigation framing attracts viewers who finish episodes. Retention is the algorithm's primary signal.

## Thumbnail Strategy

### Consistent template across all 5:
- Series branding element (logo, font, color) in the same position on every thumbnail
- One dominant image per episode (different for each)
- Minimal text — 3-5 words maximum
- High contrast for mobile visibility (most YouTube browsing is mobile)

### Suggested dominant images:
- Ep 001: Hotel hallway / trunk / FBI document aesthetic
- Ep 002: Buried window below sidewalk / underground architecture
- Ep 005: Full moon with visible surface detail / bell shape overlay
- Ep 003: LHC tunnel / particle collision visualization
- Ep 004: Old photograph aesthetic / two brothers / absence

### What NOT to do:
- Red arrows, red circles, shocked faces — signals low-quality content
- "TOP SECRET" stamps or classified document aesthetics — signals conspiracy clickbait
- Too much text — thumbnails are seen at 120x90px on mobile

After the batch drop, monitor:
- **Average view duration** — the primary signal. If episodes average 40%+ retention on 45-60 min audio, the algorithm will surface them.
- **Click-through rate** — if below 4%, titles or thumbnails need work.
