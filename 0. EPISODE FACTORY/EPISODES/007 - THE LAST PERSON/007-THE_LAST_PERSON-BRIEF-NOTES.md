# Episode 007 — THE LAST PERSON (Working Title)

## Creative Brief Notes — From GL Brainstorm 2026-04-11

### Core Concept
Something happened and now there is only one person left on earth — not because everyone died, but because everyone shifted and one person didn't. The "apocalypse" is normalcy. Everyone is fine. You're the one who's wrong.

### The Witness: Henry Collins
- Compounding pharmacist at Dandy Brook Pharmacy
- Lives alone in a densely populated area (city apartment)
- Ex-wife took their children. He's not close to them. Pictures of them all over his apartment.
- Quiet. Works, then goes home.
- Hobby: building architecture from popsicle sticks (meticulous, lonely, precise hands)
- Has addiction issues. Stole samples of a new compound that arrived at the pharmacy.

### The Drug / The Mechanism
- New compound arrives at Dandy Brook as a raw ingredient for a specialty formulation
- Henry notices something unusual about its molecular profile — a resonance, a behavior in solution that shouldn't happen
- His addiction brain asks "what would this feel like?" — he steals samples
- The compound lets him perceive/shift into other dimensions
- KEY CONCEPT: The drug didn't break his perception. It corrected it. He's briefly seeing the reality he was supposed to be in.

### The Dimensional Experience — GL's Core Vision
Henry's mundane, lonely life becomes the architecture of the other dimensions:
- His popsicle stick building appears as a skyscraper-sized version in the other dimension
- His leaky, broken faucet appears as a giant faucet in the sky that drips the same way
- Small, broken, lonely details from his personal life are the building blocks of entire realities
- The directionality is ambiguous: did he build the model because he was already seeing the skyscraper? Is his faucet broken because the cosmic faucet drips?

### The Ending — GL's Direction
Henry disappears permanently into another dimension. Cut to his ex-wife and children. They describe how he died of a drug overdose. The official story is overdose. The truth is he crossed over. The beauty of what actually happened is invisible to the people who loved him. The loneliness is THEIRS, not his anymore — and the story they carry isn't even true.
