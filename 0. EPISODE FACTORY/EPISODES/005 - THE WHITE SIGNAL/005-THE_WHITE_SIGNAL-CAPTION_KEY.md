## Notes

- Nikola: Serbian stress pattern. First syllable primary. /N IY1 K AO0 L AA0/ — NOT English /N IH0 K OW1 L AH0/.
- Varga: Hungarian surname, common in central Europe. First syllable stress. /V AA1 R G AH0/.
- Anneliese: German given name. /AE1 N EH0 L IY0 Z AH0/ — four syllables, stress on first.
- Winfried: German given name. /V IH1 N F R IY0 D/ — German "W" = English "V".
- Schumann: German surname. /SH UW1 M AH0 N/ — "Sch" = "sh" sound.
- Seifer: surname. /S AY1 F ER0/.
- Mora: surname (Portuguese/Spanish origin, used at Auckland). /M AO1 R AH0/.
- Wardenclyffe: invented place name, compound English. /W AO1 R D AH0 N K L IH2 F/.
- Smiljan: Croatian village. /S M IY1 L Y AH0 N/ — "j" = English "y".
- Westinghouse: English compound. /W EH1 S T IH0 NG HH AW2 S/.
