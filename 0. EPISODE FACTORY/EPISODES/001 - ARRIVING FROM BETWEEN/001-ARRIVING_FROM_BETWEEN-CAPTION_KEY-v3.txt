## Notes

- Nikola: Serbian stress falls on FIRST syllable. /N IY1 K AO0 L AA0/ NOT English /N IH0 K OW1 L AH0/.
- Dane: Serbian masculine name, two syllables /D AA1 N EH0/ ("DAH-neh"), NOT English monosyllable "Dane."
- Đuka: The Đ (D with stroke) is a Serbian/Croatian phoneme similar to English "D." TTS spelling Duka preserves the sound.
- Blažić: The ž is the "zh" sound (as in "measure"). TTS spelling Blazhic captures this.
- Mandić: The ć is a soft "ch." TTS spelling Mandic is an approximation.
- Gospić: The ć is a soft "ch." TTS spelling Gospic.
- Zdravko: The initial ZD cluster is a genuine Serbian consonant cluster; TTS will approximate.
- Smiljan: The j is a palatal glide ("y" sound). TTS spelling Smilyan captures this.
- Wardenclyffe: English coined name, non-obvious pronunciation. Stress on first syllable.
- Zagreb: Croatian capital. Stress on first syllable. /Z AA1 G R EH0 B/.
- Swezey: English surname, phonetically regular. /S W EH1 Z IY0/.
- Marconi removed from v3 key: name does not appear in v3 script body text.
