## CRITICAL — Verify Before Any Writing

### 1. Maurice Ewing and "rang like a bell"

**Claim in brief:** Maurice Ewing, director of the Lamont-Doherty Geological Observatory, described the Apollo twelve seismic result using the phrase "rang like a bell."

**What to verify:**
- Is Maurice Ewing the correct attribution for this phrase, or is it attributed to another NASA scientist?
- What is the exact documented phrase? The episode must use only the exact verified words in direct quotation.
- Where did Ewing (or whoever said it) say it — in testimony, a publication, an interview?

**Risk if wrong:** Defamation / misattribution to a real person. This is the episode's most prominent real-person quote. If the attribution is incorrect, the quote must be rephrased as narration without quotation marks or the attribution must be corrected.

**Suggested search:** Maurice Ewing Apollo seismic "rang like a bell"; NASA seismic experiments Apollo 12 attribution; Ewing testimony NASA Space Science Board 1970.

**Risk if wrong:** A listener who knows the actual numbers will immediately fact-check and find the error. This is the episode's cornerstone claim. Every number must be exact.
