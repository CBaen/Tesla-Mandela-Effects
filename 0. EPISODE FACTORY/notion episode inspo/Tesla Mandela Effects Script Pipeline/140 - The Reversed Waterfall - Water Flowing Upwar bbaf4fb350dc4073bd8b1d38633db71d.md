# 140 - The Reversed Waterfall - Water Flowing Upward Naturally

Alteration Type: Geographic Anomaly
Date of Occurrence: September 11, 1934
Tesla's Age at Event: 78
Cover Story: According to conventional explanations, reports of upward-flowing water are misidentifications of strong updrafts that blow spray vertically, creating the illusion of reversed flow. Geologists suggest the phenomenon is actually a series of underground channels that create artesian pressure, forcing water upward through rock fissures—unusual but not impossible. Skeptics argue that the flow is seasonal and dependent on specific weather conditions that create optical illusions. Some researchers propose the effect is caused by strong magnetic minerals in the cliff face interacting with flowing water to create apparent but not actual upward movement. The remote location and inconsistent witness reports make verification difficult. Mainstream physics dismisses claims of continuous upward water flow as violations of thermodynamics—water cannot spontaneously flow uphill without external energy input exceeding the gravitational work required. The association with Tesla is considered apocryphal, likely retroactively attributed to explain an unusual natural phenomenon through pseudoscientific legend.
Conspiracy Theory: In 1934, Tesla was determined to prove that electromagnetic forces could be harnessed to counteract gravity. His theoretical work on force field generation and wireless power had led him to understand that charged particles could be manipulated by properly configured electromagnetic fields. He knew that water molecules could be ionized through high-voltage discharge and that like charges repelled each other. His insight was to create a system that ionized falling water and then repelled it upward using an electromagnetic gradient stronger than gravity's downward pull. Between July and September 1934, he designed and constructed an ion field generator powered by the waterfall's own energy. The device used Tesla coils operating at specific frequencies to create a vertical electrostatic column. As water descended through the ionization chamber, molecules acquired positive charges. The electromagnetic field above then repelled these charged molecules with force exceeding gravitational acceleration, causing them to rise against gravity. The ascending water reached the top of the cliff and flowed over the edge again, creating a closed-loop system. The generator harvested energy from the initial descent to power the electromagnetic field, with the system producing slight excess energy—a demonstration of overunity operation through gravitational recycling. Tesla installed the generator in a remote location to avoid interference from authorities who would label the installation impossible. He documented the physics in encrypted notebooks, knowing that electromagnetic anti-gravity contradicted accepted principles. The waterfall has operated continuously for 91 years, its existence known only to occasional hikers who report the phenomenon and are dismissed as confused about natural geology. The site represents proof that electromagnetic fields can overcome gravity when applied to ionized matter.
Episode Hook: In August 2024, geologist Dr. Rachel Morrison was conducting a geological survey in the Adirondack Mountains when she encountered something that violated fundamental physics. A waterfall approximately twelve meters high was flowing upward. Not spray being blown by wind—actual liquid water ascending the cliff face in a continuous stream against gravity's pull. She confirmed her observation with instruments: flow meters showed 400 liters per minute moving vertically upward, accelerometer measurements detected upward acceleration exceeding standard gravity. She searched for pumps or hidden water sources and found neither. At the waterfall's base, she discovered an underground chamber containing equipment bearing Tesla's signature and a 1934 installation date. The chamber housed an electromagnetic generator creating intense vertical fields. High-speed cameras revealed the impossible mechanism: water molecules were being ionized as they descended, then electromagnetically repelled upward with force greater than gravity. Tesla had created an ion levitation system that had operated continuously for 91 years, recycling gravitational potential through electromagnetic manipulation. The upward waterfall was proof that matter could be made to defy gravity through field-based ion manipulation—practical electromagnetic anti-gravity operating in plain sight for nine decades.

He wanted to create undeniable physical demonstrations of principles that academia rejected as impossible. His theoretical work on force fields and electromagnetic propulsion had convinced him that gravity could be counteracted through properly configured electromagnetic systems. His goal was to create a permanent, self-sustaining demonstration that would continue operating long after his death, providing future generations with proof that electromagnetic anti-gravity was practical and real.

Process: Tesla's approach combined his deep understanding of ionization, electromagnetic field generation, and energy recycling. He selected a waterfall site specifically for its gravitational potential—falling water provided both the material to be levitated and the energy source to power the levitation system. Between July and September 1934, he designed an ion field generator using his high-frequency coil technology operating at frequencies optimized for water molecule ionization. Installation required excavating an underground chamber, waterproofing the electrical systems, and precisely calibrating field strengths to achieve stable levitation.

The closed-loop configuration recycled gravitational potential indefinitely while producing excess electrical power. This suggested a profound principle: electromagnetic forces could be used to repeatedly harvest gravitational energy from the same mass. The implications extended beyond water levitation—any matter could potentially be cycled through gravitational fields while being supported by electromagnetic forces, effectively extracting unlimited energy from gravity through field-based recycling.
