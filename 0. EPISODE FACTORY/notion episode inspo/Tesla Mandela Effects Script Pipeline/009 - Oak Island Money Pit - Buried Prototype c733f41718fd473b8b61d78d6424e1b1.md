## Hook Description

Shafts collapse. Equipment malfunctions. Deaths follow specific pattern: cardiac arrest at specific depths.

In 2019, geophysical engineer Dr. Yuki Tanaka conducts electromagnetic surveys around the pit. Expected natural geology or perhaps pirate treasure. Instead detected organized electromagnetic field patterns with specific frequencies: 7.83 Hz (Earth's Schumann resonance) and 50 kHz (Tesla coil characteristic frequency). Something 100+ feet down is generating organized EM fields. Has been continuously for over 122 years.
