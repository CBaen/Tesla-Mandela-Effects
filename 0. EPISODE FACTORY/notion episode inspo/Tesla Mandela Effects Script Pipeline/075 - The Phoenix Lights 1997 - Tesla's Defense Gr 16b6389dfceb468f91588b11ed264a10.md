Her 1948 memoir chapter on the Phoenix Lights incident preserved the most detailed firsthand account until her journal was donated to Arizona Historical Society in 1955. She died 1956, age 64, having built career on principle she learned at 28: speak truth even when punished for it, because silence enables institutional deception.
