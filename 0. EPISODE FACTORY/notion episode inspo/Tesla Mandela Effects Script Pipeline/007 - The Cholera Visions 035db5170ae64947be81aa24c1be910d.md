# 007 - The Cholera Visions

Cover Story: Fever hallucinations, delirious visions, cholera victims often experience vivid dreams. Nothing prophetic. Just illness-induced imagination.
Conspiracy Theory: In July 1873, at age 17, Nikola Tesla contracted cholera in Gospić. Medical records confirm terminal diagnosis—multiple organ failure, fever exceeding 104°F sustained for three weeks, delirium, physical shutdown. His physician Dr. Branko Stevanović documented that Tesla should have died by day 18. No patient had survived cholera at this stage in medical history.

But during the fever's peak (days 16-21), Tesla experienced something unprecedented: complete life review from birth to death at age 86. Not prophetic visions—experienced memories. He saw Wardenclyffe Tower's construction with architectural precision matching what he would later build. Witnessed Colorado Springs experiments with equipment specifications he hadn't yet conceived. Experienced his death on January 7, 1943, in room 3327 of Hotel New Yorker—details exact.

Dr. Stevanović's notes from July 28, 1873: 'Patient describes future events as memories, not predictions. Speaks of inventions in technical detail impossible for dying 17-year-old to conceive. Describes electromagnetic theory decades ahead of current science. Either delirious fabrication or he has accessed future knowledge.'

On July 29, Tesla's condition reversed impossibly. Organ function restored within 12 hours. Fever broke completely. Recovery was not gradual—it was instantaneous, as though his body received instructions to survive. Dr. Stevanović: 'Never witnessed recovery from this stage. Body responded as if future survival was predetermined fact rather than possibility.'

Tesla later told his nephew: 'I didn't survive cholera through luck. I survived because I had already lived to 86. The future was fixed. My body chose survival because the timeline required it.'
