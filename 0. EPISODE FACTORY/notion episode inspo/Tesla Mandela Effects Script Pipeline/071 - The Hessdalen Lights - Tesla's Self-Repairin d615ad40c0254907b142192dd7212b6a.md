Date of Occurrence: September 14, 1927
Tesla's Age at Event: 71
Cover Story: Hessdalen Lights are natural phenomenon - ionized dust, piezoelectric effects, ball lightning. Interesting scientific curiosity but not anomalous.

Just nothing connected to receive it. Hessdalen Lights are 98-year-old Tesla power grid maintaining itself for mission that never completed.

Ground-penetrating radar reveals extensive underground network of copper conductors in geometric patterns. Team realizes: this isn't natural. It's engineered. Power system. And it's been maintaining itself since 1927. Waiting for someone to turn on the receivers.'

Most significant: Ground analysis reveals buried power transmission equipment throughout valley, carbon-dated to 1920s. Matches Tesla's wireless power grid designs exactly. And Norwegian archives show Tesla visited region 1927, met with engineers, then abruptly left. The grid was installed. Then abandoned. But still works.

Truth: It's Tesla's 1927 wireless power grid prototype. He installed network of underground Tesla coils throughout valley. Still works. Just hasn't been connected to anything for 98 years. But it keeps running anyway. Trying to fulfill mission nobody remembers.
