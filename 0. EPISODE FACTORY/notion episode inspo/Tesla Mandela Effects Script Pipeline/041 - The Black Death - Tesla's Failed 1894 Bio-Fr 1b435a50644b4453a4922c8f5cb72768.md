# 041 - The Black Death - Tesla's Failed 1894 Bio-Frequency Cure

Alteration Type: Temporal Paradox
Date of Occurrence: November 15, 1894
Tesla's Age at Event: 38
Cover Story: Bubonic plague. Transmitted by flea bites from infected rats. Medieval poor sanitation. Natural disease progression. Completely biological with no human engineering.
Conspiracy Theory: Tesla discovered that all bacterial diseases can be destroyed with specific electromagnetic frequencies - no drugs needed. In 1894, he perfected the technique. He attempted to send the knowledge back to 1347 to prevent the Black Death. His consciousness transferred successfully. But when he tried to teach frequency healing, he was declared a witch. His host was burned alive with the frequency knowledge. Tesla snapped back to 1894, traumatized. But while there, he discovered the plague itself showed signs of artificial frequency manipulation - it was engineered. Someone from the future created the Black Death deliberately. Population control before fossil fuels. Tesla realized: some disasters MUST happen. He never published his frequency healing cure.
Episode Hook: November 15, 1894: Czito finds Tesla screaming in his laboratory. Tesla has burns on his arms that weren't there an hour ago. 'I was there. 1347. They burned me alive. I tried to save them. I had the cure. The frequency to destroy the plague bacteria. They thought it was witchcraft. But Czito - the plague itself is unnatural. Someone MADE it. Someone from the future weaponized Yersinia pestis and released it in 1347. I found evidence. Frequency signatures. The Black Death was deliberate. And I can't stop it because if I do, industrial revolution happens 200 years early. Nuclear weapons by 1750. This is why I can't save them.'
Evidence It Was Different: Modern genetic analysis of Black Death bacteria from 1340s shows impossible mutation patterns - specifically optimized for human transmission. DNA shows signs of frequency manipulation at the molecular level. No natural evolution produces these patterns. Historical records mention 'strange lights' and 'humming sounds' in years before outbreak. Medieval manuscripts describe healers who knew 'singing cure' - specific tones that helped patients recover. These healers were systematically eliminated. Tesla's 1894 notebooks contain precise frequencies for destroying 47 different bacteria - including Yersinia pestis. The frequency matches medieval descriptions of the 'singing cure.'
What Changed: 1347-1353: The Black Death kills 200 million people across Europe. History says it was bubonic plague from fleas. But in 1894, Tesla successfully developed bioelectric frequency healing. He tested it by sending his consciousness back to 1347 with knowledge of the precise frequency that destroys Yersinia pestis bacteria. He tried to teach medieval healers. They interpreted it as witchcraft. Burned his host body at the stake. The frequency knowledge died with him. Tesla returned to 1894 having failed to prevent the plague. But he learned something worse: The Black Death was itself a bioweapon created by future time travelers trying to reduce Earth's population before the industrial revolution.
The Twist: In 1942, one year before Tesla's death, he finally understood: He wasn't supposed to stop the Black Death. He was supposed to CAUSE it. His failed attempt in 1347 created a paradox. The time loop requires someone from the future to engineer the plague. That someone is Tesla. In his final journals: 'I have the frequency to create weaponized Yersinia pestis. I have the time travel capability. The loop demands I go back to 1346 and release it. 200 million must die to prevent 4 billion from dying in 1750s nuclear war. I thought I was trying to save them. I was actually supposed to kill them. And I can't do it. So the loop remains incomplete. Someone else will close it. God forgive whoever does.'
