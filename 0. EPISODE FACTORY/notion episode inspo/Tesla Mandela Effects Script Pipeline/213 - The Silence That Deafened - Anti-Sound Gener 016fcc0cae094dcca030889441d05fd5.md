Tesla's Journey: ## Motivation

## Process

Between September 1867 and June 1869, Tesla conducted systematic experiments mapping his silence zones. He discovered the effect was stronger indoors (sound waves reflected off walls into his interference zone) and weaker outdoors (open spaces allowed sound to propagate around him before encountering anti-waves).

He learned optimal positioning: stay at edges of rooms, avoid enclosed spaces, maintain distance from sound sources so their waves didn't enter his interference radius before reaching other listeners. He became expert at positioning himself to minimize others' perception of the effect.

He studied resonance: certain frequencies penetrated his anti-wave field more effectively. Low frequencies (below 60 Hz) and very high frequencies (above 12 kHz) showed reduced cancellation. He learned to listen for these frequencies, using them to partially maintain acoustic awareness.

By 1869, Tesla realized the silence zones would persist his entire life. Unlike phase shifting (controllable through concentration), the anti-acoustic field generation was automatic and unconscious. His atomic structure constantly generated inverse waveforms. No amount of mental discipline could stop it.

Worse: he realized the effect wasn't limited to sound. Light was slightly dimmer near him. Heat transfer was slightly reduced. Everything wave-based was being partially canceled. His body was creating a localized zone of suppressed physics. This was more fundamental and more disturbing than phase shifting.
