Cover Story: The San Francisco earthquake of April 18, 1906 was a natural tectonic event caused by rupture along the San Andreas fault. The earthquake resulted from accumulated stress between the Pacific and North American plates reaching failure threshold. Seismologists have thoroughly documented the earthquake through contemporary seismographic records, eyewitness accounts, and geological surveys of surface rupture. The magnitude 7.9 earthquake and subsequent fires killed an estimated 3,000 people and destroyed 80 percent of San Francisco. No evidence exists for any human involvement or technological triggering mechanism. Claims about Tesla causing the earthquake are pseudoscientific conspiracy theories unsupported by historical documentation. Tesla was in New York during the earthquake and had no means of remotely triggering seismic events. His earthquake machine experiments were small-scale laboratory demonstrations using mechanical oscillators that could not transmit energy across continental distances. Telegraph systems of 1906 lacked capacity to transmit sufficient power for seismic triggering. Tesla's supposed destroyed equipment and guilt-ridden diary entries are fabrications created by conspiracy theorists decades after both the earthquake and Tesla's death. The conspiracy theory persists because of human tendency to seek agency behind natural disasters and because it misattributes revolutionary scientist status to Tesla beyond his actual accomplishments.

Tesla's failed humanitarian test became the foundation for seismic warfare.
