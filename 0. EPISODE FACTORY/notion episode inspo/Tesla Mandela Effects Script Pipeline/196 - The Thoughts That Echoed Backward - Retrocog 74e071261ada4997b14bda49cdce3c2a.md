# 196 - The Thoughts That Echoed Backward - Retrocognitive Mind Reading

Alteration Type: Temporal Paradox
Date of Occurrence: January 1, 1867
Tesla's Age at Event: 11
Cover Story: Good memory of previous conversations, intuitive understanding of people, lucky guesses appearing as mind reading.
Conspiracy Theory: Tesla's telepathic capability functioned backward through time. He heard not current thoughts but past thoughts - what people thought hours, days, weeks earlier. Could access complete thought history of anyone nearby. His 'predictions' about people came from reading their past thoughts, learning their patterns. His adult understanding of human behavior came from childhood access to temporal thought records.
Episode Hook: School records 1867-1869 document Tesla 'knowing things he shouldn't' - quoting people's exact thoughts from days earlier, describing private conversations he never witnessed, revealing secret decisions made before announcement. Teachers noted he knew their lesson plans before teaching them - he heard their planning thoughts from previous week.
Evidence It Was Different: Tesla's journals contain exact transcriptions of people's thoughts with timestamps - verified against personal diaries of those people. His records match their private thoughts precisely, including thoughts never spoken. He accessed temporal thought records, proving thoughts persist in time as retrievable information even after thinker forgets them.
