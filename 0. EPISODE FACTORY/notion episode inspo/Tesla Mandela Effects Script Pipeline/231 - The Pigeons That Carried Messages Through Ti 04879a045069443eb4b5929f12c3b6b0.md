Character 2 - Historical Associate: Full Name: Kolman Czito (Tesla's laboratory assistant)

Real Association: Tesla's most trusted assistant who worked with him for decades, documented in historical records.

Relationship to Tesla: Loyal assistant who witnessed pigeon temporal experiments firsthand and maintained detailed laboratory journals.

Role in Discovery: Present during August 1926 experiments when Tesla first achieved pigeon temporal displacement. Czito documented white pigeon vanishing mid-flight through window, reappearing 15 minutes later with note attached showing future date. Conducted systematic testing over following months, recording temporal coordinates and message contents.

Personal Conflict: As practical engineer, believed in cause-effect linearity. Pigeons traveling through time violated his understanding of physics. Witnessing bird disappear in 1926 and return with note dated 1947 shattered his mechanistic worldview.

Emotional Stakes: Lost certainty about time's unidirectional flow. Gained documentation of biological time travel that would revolutionize physics if revealed. Fear that publishing would destroy both his and Tesla's reputations.

Character Arc: From practical assistant focused on building Tesla's inventions, to witness of temporal impossibility. Maintained secret documentation for decades, published posthumously by his family in 1968.
