Modern scholars debate whether the famous Library of Alexandria burned completely in 48 BCE or suffered gradual decline over subsequent centuries through multiple smaller fires, budget cuts, and neglect. Most historians view the library's loss as a complex process rather than a single event caused by a single agent.

Character 4 - Ordinary Person: ## Full Name
Maria Kowalski

## Role
Tesla's housekeeper and cook at his Manhattan residence, 1890-1900

## Background
Polish immigrant, age 34 in 1891. Practical, observant woman who managed Tesla's household while he worked obsessively in his laboratory. Trusted with Tesla's daily routines, health monitoring, and basic recordkeeping. No scientific training but possessive of common sense and sharp observational skills.

## Personal Stakes
Maria witnessed Tesla's psychological deterioration across forty-seven time travel attempts. Each projection left him physically weakened and emotionally devastated for days. She documented his declining health, weight loss, and increasing dissociation from present reality. Her employment and family's financial stability depended on Tesla maintaining functional mental state.

She faced moral dilemma: report Tesla's dangerous experiments to authorities (potentially getting him institutionalized), or remain silent and watch him destroy himself. She chose protection through documentation—keeping detailed daily journals recording Tesla's condition, hoping someone would eventually intervene before he killed himself through temporal projection.

## Connection to Discovery
Maria's journals provided most detailed account of Project Hindsight's psychological costs. While Czito documented technical aspects and Tesla recorded temporal observations, Maria recorded the human impact. Her entries describe Tesla's guilt spirals, his recurring nightmares about burning scrolls, his increasing isolation from human connection as temporal loop obsession consumed him.

She never understood the physics but recognized destructive obsession. Her journal entry from April 1891: "Mr. Tesla believes he has burned an ancient library with his mind. I don't know if this is real or delusion. I know it is killing him either way. Someone should make him stop."

## Character Arc
Beginning (1890): Straightforward housekeeper who viewed her employer as eccentric genius requiring basic life management. Believed science was incomprehensible but harmless.

Transformation (1891-1900): Forced to witness how pursuit of knowledge without regard for psychological cost destroys brilliant minds. Learned that understanding whether something is scientifically real matters less than recognizing when it's psychologically destructive.

End (1900): Left Tesla's employment after Project Hindsight's final attempt, unable to continue witnessing his decline. Her departure letter: "I cannot watch you die slowly from guilt over something you may have caused or may have imagined. Real or not, it is destroying you. I cannot help you anymore." Spent remaining years (1900-1932) working for families of scientists, becoming informal advocate for researchers' mental health. Her published memoir (1928) included encrypted descriptions of Project Hindsight, warning about obsession's dangers in experimental research. Died believing the truth of Tesla's time travel mattered less than the reality of its psychological destruction of a good man.
