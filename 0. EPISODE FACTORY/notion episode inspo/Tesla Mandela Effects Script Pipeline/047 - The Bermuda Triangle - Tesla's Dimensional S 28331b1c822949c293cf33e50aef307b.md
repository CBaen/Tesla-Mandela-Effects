# 047 - The Bermuda Triangle - Tesla's Dimensional Scar

Alteration Type: Dimensional Breach
Date of Occurrence: July 15, 1898
Tesla's Age at Event: 42
Cover Story: Bermuda Triangle disappearances are combination of heavy traffic, tropical weather, human error, and media exaggeration. Most incidents have rational explanations. No mysterious force required.
Conspiracy Theory: 1898: Tesla conducts dimensional rift experiments in Caribbean waters using a ship-mounted generator. Goal: create portal for instant transportation. Portal opens successfully. But the closing mechanism fails. Portal stabilizes in semi-open state - flickering between closed and open states randomly. Testing ship enters portal during 'open' state. Disappears. Never returns. Tesla tries to close portal. Can't. It's locked in quantum superposition - simultaneously open and closed. Ships passing through during 'open' phase enter the portal and don't come back. Tesla marks coordinates as 'dimensional hazard zone' and leaves. By 1950s, dozens of disappearances. Area gets nicknamed Bermuda Triangle. But it's not a triangle. It's a circle around Tesla's broken portal. And it still flickers open randomly, consuming whatever passes through.
Episode Hook: December 5, 1945: Flight 19, five Navy bombers, vanishes in Bermuda Triangle. Last transmission: 'Everything looks strange, even the ocean... We can't tell where we are... It looks like we're entering white water.' Rescue plane sent to find them also disappears. 27 men gone. But Navy recordings include 2 more seconds of transmission that were classified: 'There's a massive opening in the ocean... looks like gateway... ship passing through ahead of us... it's not water, it's...' Transmission ends. Classified because they saw Tesla's portal. And flew into it.
Evidence It Was Different: Bermuda Triangle disappearances cluster around specific coordinates - not random across the whole 'triangle'. Modern ships report compass malfunctions, electromagnetic interference, and 'rippling air' at exact center point. Survivors describe seeing 'hole in the ocean' or 'sky tearing open' before ships vanish. Most damning: Tesla's 1898 Caribbean expedition logs, found in his confiscated papers, describe dimensional rift test at 25.0°N 71.0°W - exact center of Bermuda Triangle. His last entry: 'Portal will not close. Oscillates between states. Recommend avoiding coordinates. Potential casualty zone for centuries.' He was right.
What Changed: The Bermuda Triangle has claimed 50+ ships and 20+ aircraft since 1800s. Official explanation: natural magnetic anomalies, human error, weather. Truth: In 1898, Tesla tested his first dimensional portal generator in the Caribbean. The test succeeded - opened a portal to somewhere else. But Tesla couldn't close it. The rift stabilized at specific coordinates: 25°N 71°W - center of what would become known as the Bermuda Triangle. The portal doesn't open fully. It flickers. Ships pass through during flickers and never return. The Bermuda Triangle isn't mysterious weather. It's Tesla's broken dimensional doorway, still randomly opening 127 years later, still consuming anything that passes through at the wrong moment.
The Twist: The portal doesn't lead to another dimension. It leads to another TIME. Ships don't disappear - they time-travel. Flight 19 emerged in 1898, before they took off. They crashed in Caribbean, were found by Tesla's expedition, and Tesla realized: his portal creates closed timelike curves. The planes that went missing in 1945 crashed in 1898. The 1898 expedition that found crashed planes from 1945 created the portal that caused them to crash in 1898. The Bermuda Triangle is a causality loop. Everything that disappears went back in time and caused the portal that made them disappear. Tesla documented 27 anachronistic crashes between 1898-1900. All from the future. All caused by the portal they traveled through. And he couldn't close it without erasing their time travel - which would erase the evidence that led him to create the portal.
