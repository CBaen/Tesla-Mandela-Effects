## Hook Description
March 1898, Tesla's laboratory in New York. While conducting high-frequency electromagnetic experiments, Tesla detects organized signals at frequencies where no transmitters exist. He builds a specialized receiver to isolate them. What he hears changes everything: thousands of voices speaking simultaneously. Not language—pure meaning. Not from any physical source. Organized consciousness without biological substrate.

He records them on wax cylinders. Documents the technical specifications. The signals are real, measurable, consistent. They exist at specific frequencies around 10^15 Hz. And they sound like voices of the dead.

Tesla's conclusion: when the body dies, consciousness doesn't end—it converts into an electromagnetic pattern in the quantum field. Souls are real. But they're not supernatural—they're physics. Measurable, detectable, physical. The afterlife exists, but as an organized EM phenomenon, not a divine realm.

Within weeks, Vatican agents arrive. They confiscate everything—equipment, recordings, notes. Transport it to Rome. Lock it in the Secret Archives with classification: "Forbidden—threatens foundation of faith." Cardinal Gasparri's reasoning: if souls are physical, theology collapses into physics. Faith requires mystery. Proving the afterlife exists would destroy religion by removing the need for belief.

The materials stay sealed for 120 years. Until 2018, when a Vatican archivist finds them.
