# 085 - The Plague Doctor Sightings - Time-Displaced Tesla Agents

Date of Occurrence: June 18, 1942

Historical records show plague doctors used techniques unknown in medieval era. Because they were from future. Tesla's first successful humanitarian time mission. Proved time travel can change history for better.

And mortality statistics show inexplicable improvement 1348-1353, returning to normal 1354. Temporal intervention explains all anomalies. Agents from 1942, saving medieval victims, then returning to their time.
