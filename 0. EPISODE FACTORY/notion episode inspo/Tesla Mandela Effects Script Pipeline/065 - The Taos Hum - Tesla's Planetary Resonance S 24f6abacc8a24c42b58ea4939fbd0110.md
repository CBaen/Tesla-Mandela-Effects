Spent 15 years (1991-2006) documenting the phenomenon while being treated as either mentally ill or attention-seeking by skeptics. Dr. Corum's 2006 paper revealing electromagnetic origin vindicated her: she'd been detecting Tesla's planetary resonance. Ended life (died 2018, age 79) having shifted from dismissed 'hum sufferer' to recognized documentarian of planetary electromagnetic phenomenon. Her legacy: the ordinary person whose persistence in reporting 'impossible' experience eventually led to understanding of century-old Tesla technology still operating.
