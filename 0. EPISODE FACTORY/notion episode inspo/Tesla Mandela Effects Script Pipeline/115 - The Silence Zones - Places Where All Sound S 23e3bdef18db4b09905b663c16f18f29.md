Hook Description: October 31, 1901, Wardenclyffe Laboratory, Long Island. Tesla dismisses as superstition, investigates personally. Enters northwest sector carrying bell, matches, metal tools. Strikes bell—no sound. Drops wrench—silent impact. Shouts—hears nothing. Steps backward three feet—sounds return immediately. Bell rings, wrench clangs, voice echoes normally.

Phenomenon boundary is sharp. Tesla marks perimeter with chalk. Zone measures roughly 12x15 feet. He tests extensively: pocket watch makes no ticking inside zone. Gunshot produces recoil but no sound. Even his own heartbeat becomes inaudible when hand pressed to chest within boundary.

Tesla's immediate hypothesis: his tower's high-frequency electromagnetic transmissions are creating standing wave interference patterns that disrupt acoustic wave propagation. Not sound absorption—acoustic cancellation through EM disruption of air molecule vibration.

Tesla spends November 1901 mapping zones. They appear and disappear, correlating precisely with tower transmission schedules. When tower operates at specific frequencies, zones manifest. When transmission stops, zones vanish. Pattern confirms hypothesis: his EM fields are creating regions where sound cannot exist.

December 1901: Tesla engineers first controlled silence zone. Continuous low-power transmission maintains stable acoustic null space in basement laboratory. He creates three permanent installations—rooms where no sound can exist. Workers refuse to enter them. Tesla conducts experiments alone.

January 1902: After spending 6+ hours in silence zone conducting tests, Tesla experiences disturbing revelation. Without external sounds, he begins hearing internal ones—rhythmic patterns, voices, music that can't be real because no acoustic energy exists in the zone.
