## Thumbnail System — The TME Standard

Every thumbnail follows the same visual rules:

1. **One hero image** — full bleed, from the episode's AI-generated cinematography
2. **Kodak Vision3 500T color grade** — warm tungsten amber, consistent across all episodes
3. **Text:** 3 words maximum, weighted serif font, cream/gold on dark
4. **Text position:** Lower-left or centered bottom — SAME position every episode
5. **No faces with exaggerated expressions** — you are not clickbait
6. **No collages** — one dominant image per thumbnail
7. **Specs:** 1280x720px, 16:9, JPG, under 2MB
8. **Use YouTube's Test & Compare** to A/B test 2-3 variations per episode
