### External Official YouTube / Google Sources

Sources checked:

- YouTube Partner Program overview and eligibility: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851
- YouTube channel monetization policies: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- YouTube upload and Checks docs: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407

- YouTube mid-roll docs: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006
- YouTube notification docs: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7457584
- YouTube altered/synthetic content docs: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Google video structured data docs: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video

Findings:

- To join full ad-revenue YPP, the current official threshold is 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- YouTube reviews the channel as a whole, including main theme, newest videos, biggest watch-time videos, metadata, thumbnails, descriptions, and About section.
- Monetization reviewers expect original, authentic content that is not mass-produced or repetitive. TME's advantage is that each episode is original long-form story content, not reused footage or template-only narration.
- For monetized uploads, YouTube recommends using Checks before publishing and waiting for a green/yellow/red decision before going public. For maximum revenue, do not publish while the icon is still `Checking`.
- Self-Certification matters because accurate ratings can improve future monetization decision speed and reliability.
- Mid-roll ads are available for monetized videos 8 minutes or longer. Manual slots should be placed at natural breaks, such as pauses in audio or visual transitions. Manual plus automatic slots can increase ad opportunities, but slots are not guaranteed to serve ads.
- YouTube's altered/synthetic-content disclosure is required when content seems realistic and is meaningfully altered or generated. The TME visual/narration style should disclose synthetic/altered content to avoid viewer-trust and policy risk.
- Google video SEO rewards dedicated watch pages, stable thumbnail URLs, consistent metadata, and YouTube description timestamps for key moments.

### External Market Reality

Public CPM/RPM estimates by niche are noisy and should not be treated as a forecast. The stable pattern across creator-economy sources is:

- finance, insurance, legal, business, software, and real estate usually monetize higher than entertainment;
- broad entertainment usually monetizes lower;
- history/documentary/mystery sits in the middle and depends heavily on viewer country, age, retention, topic, advertiser demand, and brand-safety classification;
- long-form videos have more monetization opportunity than short videos because they can support mid-roll ad opportunities.

Practical implication:

TME should not pretend to be finance or education just to chase CPM. The correct high-value lane is `cinematic historical mystery / science-history docufiction for adults`, with professional metadata, clean disclaimers, and no low-trust conspiracy posture.

## Convergence

Internal and external evidence converge on the same operating model:

- Brand as original historical docufiction.
- Use real historical entities for indexing.
- Keep speculative/fabricated material clearly framed as storytelling.
- Avoid conspiracy identity language.
- Keep thumbnails cinematic, not tabloid.
- Use upload checks, accurate self-certification, and synthetic-content disclosure.
- Use mid-rolls deliberately, at natural breaks, after upload processing.

## Divergence

- The older local guide recommends Premieres, but current YouTube help says output above 1080p is not supported for Premieres. For the current 1440p masters, scheduled public uploads are safer unless separate 1080p Premiere files are made.
- Some older internal language uses `hidden history`, `mud flood`, and similar audience-capture terms. For ad value and channel review, those should be reduced in metadata and replaced with entity-rich history/storytelling language.
- The internal SEO doc leans into ARG/unfiction language. That helps safety, but the public brand should remain simpler and more professional: `historical docufiction`, `cinematic historical mystery`, `speculative reconstruction`.

## Recommendation

For the first two episodes, use the current titles, but upgrade the preparation around them:

- Upload as private/scheduled.
- Turn monetization on only after the channel is eligible and modules are accepted.
- Use accurate altered/synthetic disclosure.
- Wait for Checks to complete before publishing.
- If monetized, turn on mid-rolls and use manual plus automatic ad slots.
- Keep metadata entity-rich and ad-safe.
- Remove `mud flood`, `cover-up`, `exposed`, `truth`, and political accusation terms from tags/descriptions.
- Public-facing copy should never describe fictional documents as verified real-world evidence. Use `inside the story`, `archive fragment`, `speculative reconstruction`, or `docufiction` language when the source is invented.
- Use companion pages and structured data after public URLs exist.
- Treat YouTube Studio's monetization feedback as evidence. If a video gets yellow, do not publish first and fix later; either appeal, adjust metadata, or re-upload after deciding.
