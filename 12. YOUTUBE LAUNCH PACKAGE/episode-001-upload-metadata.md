## Description

Nikola Tesla died in Room 3327 of the Hotel New Yorker on January 7, 1943. This historical docufiction episode follows the last room backward through Smiljan, childhood visions, the white pigeon, and the question of what the archive can and cannot prove.

January 7th, 1943. Room 3327, Hotel New Yorker. A maid named Alice Monaghan unlocked the door after three days of silence. The body was cold. The sheets were pulled military-precise. And the air smelled like the sky after a lightning strike - in a sealed room, in January, on the thirty-third floor.

This is the story of Nikola Tesla's life told backward from the room where it ended - through the geometric visions that replaced his sight as a child, the sparks he pulled from a cat's back at age three, the lightning storm in Smiljan, the pigeons, and the government office that arrived after death.

The question is not only how Tesla died. The question is what the archive could not hold.
