## Current YouTube / Google Platform Rules Checked

- YouTube upload help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407`): desktop upload supports multiple files, title limit is 100 characters, description limit is 5,000 characters, checks can run before publishing, and upload details include captions, category, distribution, notifications, tags, monetization, ad suitability, and chapters.
- YouTube Partner Program eligibility (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851`): full ad-revenue YPP eligibility currently requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- YouTube channel monetization policies (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392`): reviewers look at the channel as a whole, including main theme, newest videos, biggest watch-time videos, metadata, thumbnails, descriptions, and About section; original/authentic content is favored over repetitive or reused content.
- YouTube advertiser-friendly content guidelines (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278`): monetization suitability applies to the video, thumbnail, title, description, and tags.
- YouTube Self-Certification overview (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7687980`): accurate self-ratings can help monetization decisions become faster and more reliable.
- YouTube mid-roll help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006`): videos 8 minutes or longer can use mid-rolls; manual slots work best at natural audio/visual breaks; manual plus automatic slots can increase ad opportunities, but no slot is guaranteed to serve.
- YouTube account verification help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171664`): phone verification enables uploads longer than 15 minutes and custom thumbnails.
- YouTube notification help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7457584`): viewers get a maximum of 3 upload/live stream notifications from a channel in 24 hours.
- YouTube Premiere help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9080341`): scheduled Premieres create public watch pages and reminder notifications, but output greater than 1080p is not supported for Premieres.
- YouTube chapters help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579`): manual chapters need a first timestamp at 00:00, at least three timestamps, ascending order, and chapter length of at least 10 seconds.
- YouTube tags help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402`): title, thumbnail, and description are more important than tags; tags mainly help with misspellings.
- YouTube hashtags help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658`): hashtags can be added to title/description; up to three prominent hashtags can show by the title.
- YouTube altered/synthetic content help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491`): realistic generated scenes or alterations that could seem real require disclosure, and disclosure does not limit audience or monetization eligibility by itself.
- YouTube custom thumbnail help (`https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431`): recommended 1280x720, JPG/GIF/PNG, under 2MB for videos, and 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Google video structured data (`https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video`): if a companion website exists, use a dedicated indexed watch page, stable thumbnail URL, consistent metadata, VideoObject structured data, and key moments.
