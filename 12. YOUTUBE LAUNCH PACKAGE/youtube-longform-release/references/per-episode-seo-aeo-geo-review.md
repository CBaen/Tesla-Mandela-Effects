# Per-Episode SEO/AEO/GEO Review

## Purpose

Review each longform episode as its own search and answer surface. Do not only
apply a shared batch template. The three videos share a brand, but each episode
needs its own title, entity map, answer blocks, chapter quality, and
monetization risk review.

## Review Order

1. Watch or sample the actual render enough to confirm the premise, tone, and
   content risks.
2. Read the existing upload metadata file.
3. Read the monetization prep file.
4. Inspect the thumbnail file.
5. Inspect captions/transcript for entity spelling and fabricated-source risk.
6. Compare against current YouTube guidance and current public search context.
7. Produce exact edits, not vague advice.

## SEO Review

Check:

- title names the central entity and episode hook;
- thumbnail promise matches the title and first minute;
- first two description lines state exact subject, genre, and premise;
- tags are misspellings, alternate names, and entity variants only;
- chapters start at `00:00`, have at least three ascending timestamps, and use
  clear search-friendly labels;
- playlist placement supports binge/session time.

## AEO Review

Add answer-shaped clarity for viewers and AI answer systems:

- "What is this episode about?"
- "Is this factual documentary or docufiction?"
- "Which real history appears?"
- "Which parts are speculative or fictionalized?"
- "Where should a new viewer start?"

Use direct answers. Do not over-explain the technology of AEO in the public
copy.

## GEO Review

Make the episode easy for generative search and companion pages:

- stable exact title;
- concise summary;
- key entity list;
- cleaned transcript or excerpt plan;
- clear docufiction disclaimer;
- structured companion page plan after watch URL exists;
- consistent playlist/channel naming.

Do not invent YouTube watch URLs or VideoObject IDs before upload creates them.
