## Process Notes for Future Instances

- **The single-session model worked.** Brief → 3 subagent writing passes → verification → fixes → Bible update. No handoff needed. No expansion passes. No multi-round editor review.
- **Fact verification in parallel with Pass 1 is efficient.** Found the tower height error before it could compound through later passes. Launch verification early.
- **The "feel/felt" cluster teaches a lesson:** intentional rhetorical repetition ("It would feel like...") is fine per the Production Guide, but extending the same word into the NEXT paragraph tips from rhythm into clustering. Future instances: be aware of where intentional anaphora ends and accidental clustering begins.
- **Guiding Light's creative direction was minimal and brilliant:** "more awe... but joyous... make it up... proving we're in the different universe!" That's all it took. Trust the direction and run with it.
