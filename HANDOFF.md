## Episode 002 Key Creative Decisions

- **Structural shape:** The Descent (not Crime Scene Investigation, which was blocked by 008)
- **Closing type:** The Invitation (find the oldest building on your street, look at the foundation) — same type as 008, but this session predates the window rule taking effect; 002 was written before 008 existed in production order
- **Emotional endpoint:** Quiet acceptance — the investigation closes without verdict, listener holds the shape of the gap
- **Tesla connection:** Experiment consequence — polyphase AC running through unknown materials for six months; the implication is structural, never stated as conclusion
- **The planted image:** "a pan that rang like a bell" appears in the Wound (paragraph 19), is quoted in its full form mid-investigation, and pays off in the final Return section ("This wall gives back more than it should. Like ringing a pan and hearing a bell.")
- **Three-source convergence:** Coburn (1894), Halsted/Briggs incident report (1894), Alcott (1904) — independent, ignorant of each other, same anomaly. This is the series' evidence architecture in its cleanest form.
- **The grade raise:** Chicago's 1850s-1860s civic grade raise used as structural parallel — buried city under the new city, a pattern of encountering anomalous material in subsurface operations. Grounds the "something older" implication in documented civic history.
