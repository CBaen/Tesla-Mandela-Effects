# Episode Metadata For Rumble And Spotify

## Shared Rules

- Correct Episode 003 to `S1E3`.
- Lead with the mystery and the exact searchable entities.
- Keep the docufiction disclaimer visible, but do not let it smother the hook.
- Do not overuse hashtags.
- Do not describe fictional archive fragments as verified real sources.
- Treat tags as entity/misspelling support, plus a few audience-intent terms.
- Favor `Nikola Tesla`, `Mandela Effect`, `historical mystery`, `lost papers`,
  `alternate history`, and exact place/event/entity names over generic
  compliance language.
