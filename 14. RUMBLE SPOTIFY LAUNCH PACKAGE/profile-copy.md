Tesla Mandela Effects is a feature-length historical mystery series about
Nikola Tesla, Mandela Effects, missing records, impossible architecture,
electrical anomalies, and the places where the archive stops answering.

Sharper positioning is allowed. Lead with mystery, history, Tesla, memory,
science, archives, and anomalies. Keep the docufiction boundary present, but do
not make the first impression sound like legal copy.

sources are verified, do not call the episodes factual documentaries, and do
